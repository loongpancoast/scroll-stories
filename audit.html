Screen 01 — Thoracic rotation

Stretching is not screening.

Stretching what feels tight is not the same as screening what restricts. The audit finds the actual limiter behind your swing fault, then gives you drills for that limiter only.

If your slice comes from your body stalling in the downswing, you will see exactly where. If it is a club path issue instead, it will tell you that too — so you stop chasing the wrong fix.

  • Seated rotation test — left vs right, measured in degrees
  • Overhead squat — where the chain actually breaks
  • Single-leg balance — the quiet killer of consistency
Screen 02 — Hip hinge

Your body is the first coach.

Every swing fault has a body behind it. Early extension, loss of posture, the slide — these are not swing problems first. They are movement problems wearing a swing costume.

The audit films two screens, scores them against the patterns that actually correlate with ball-striking, and hands you the three drills that move your number — not a generic mobility program.

  • Hip hinge screen — can you load without losing spine angle
  • Personal drill prescription — three moves, not thirty
  • Re-screen in 30 days — the number moves or the plan changes
